PART I - FINANCIAL INFORMATION

Item 1. Financial Statements

The condensed consolidated balance sheets as of July 26, 2026 and January 25, 2026, and the
related condensed consolidated statements of income for the quarter then ended, are included
herein. These statements are unaudited and reflect all adjustments that management considers
necessary for a fair statement of the periods presented.

Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 was 41.3 billion, up 19 percent from a year
earlier, driven by demand for accelerated computing platforms across cloud and sovereign
customers. Data center revenue grew 24 percent year over year, while gaming revenue was
roughly flat. Gross margin was 71.2 percent, down 40 basis points sequentially on product
mix. Operating expenses rose as the company ramped production of its next-generation
accelerators and expanded its systems software organization.

Item 3. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

The company is exposed to interest rate risk on its investment portfolio and to foreign
currency exchange risk on sales denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar. A
hypothetical 10 percent movement in exchange rates would not have a material impact on
results. There were no material changes to the market risk disclosures in the most recent
annual report.

PART II - OTHER INFORMATION

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Supply of advanced packaging capacity remains concentrated among a small number of vendors,
and any disruption could delay shipments of the company's data center products. Export
control regulations continue to evolve, and additional restrictions could reduce addressable
demand in certain regions. Customer concentration also remains elevated, with the top four
cloud customers representing a significant share of data center revenue.
