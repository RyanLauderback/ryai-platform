PART I - FINANCIAL INFORMATION

Item 1. Financial Statements

The unaudited consolidated financial statements of the firm as of June 30, 2026 and for the
three and six months then ended are included herein, together with the notes thereto. In the
opinion of management, all adjustments necessary for a fair presentation have been made.

Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The firm reported net income of 14.9 billion for the second quarter of 2026, with net
interest income modestly higher as loan growth offset narrower deposit spreads. Markets
revenue rose 11 percent on stronger activity in rates and currencies. The provision for
credit losses was 2.1 billion, reflecting a stable consumer credit environment and a small
reserve build in wholesale lending. The common equity tier 1 capital ratio was 15.8 percent.

Item 3. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

The firm's market risk profile was broadly unchanged during the quarter. Average value at
risk for the trading portfolio was within the range disclosed in the most recent annual
report, and stress loss estimates remained within established limits.

PART II - OTHER INFORMATION

Item 1A. Risk Factors

There have been no material changes to the risk factors previously disclosed in the firm's
annual report for the year ended December 31, 2025.
