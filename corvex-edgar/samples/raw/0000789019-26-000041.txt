PART I

Item 1. Business

The company develops and supports software, services, devices, and solutions worldwide. Its
segments include productivity and business processes, intelligent cloud, and more personal
computing. During fiscal 2026 the company continued to invest in cloud infrastructure and in
applied artificial intelligence across its product families, and it completed the build-out
of three additional datacenter regions.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The company's cloud services depend on the availability of datacenter capacity, electricity,
and networking, and capacity constraints could limit growth. Competition in productivity
software and in cloud infrastructure remains intense. Regulatory proceedings in several
jurisdictions could result in fines or changes to business practices, and evolving rules on
the export of artificial intelligence services add compliance complexity.

PART II

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for fiscal 2026 was 288.4 billion, an increase of 14 percent over fiscal 2025,
reflecting growth in cloud services and in subscription productivity offerings. Operating
income grew 16 percent as gross margin expansion in the cloud segment offset continued
infrastructure investment. The company returned 38 billion to shareholders through dividends
and share repurchases during the year.

Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

The consolidated financial statements, together with the report of the independent
registered public accounting firm, are included herein, including the consolidated balance
sheets as of June 30, 2026 and 2025 and the related statements of income, comprehensive
income, cash flows, and stockholders' equity for each of the three years ended June 30,
2026.
